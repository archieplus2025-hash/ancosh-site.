# Updating the website (for Council editors)

1. Go to **https://ancosh.org/admin** and choose **Login with GitHub**. You need a GitHub account that the site owner has added to the project.
2. Pick what you want to change: **News**, **Events**, **Leadership**, **Resources**, or **Contact details and social links**.
3. Press **New News story** (or Event, Person, Resource), fill in the form, and press **Publish**. Use the Dari fields if you want a Dari version; if left empty, the English shows.
4. Wait about a minute. The site rebuilds itself and your change goes live.

Notes
- Events disappear from the site automatically after their date.
- To remove something, open it and choose **Delete entry**.
- Photos and files you upload are stored with the site. Keep photos under 1 MB.
- Programs, About and Culture page text is not editable here yet. Ask the web developer.
- Messages from the Contact form arrive in the Netlify dashboard (Forms) and can be emailed to dehati@ancosh.org.
