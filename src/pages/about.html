About the Council

A community organization dedicated to the identity, culture, unity, and social engagement of Afghanistan's Sunni Hazaras.

Our mission

The National Council of Sunni Hazaras of Afghanistan is committed to preserving, strengthening, and promoting the ethnic, cultural, and social identity of Afghanistan's Sunni Hazara community.

The Council works to safeguard the community's cultural and historical heritage, strengthen solidarity and cooperation among Hazaras, advocate for the legitimate rights and interests of the community, and create opportunities for their constructive participation in society.

The Council also promotes unity, peaceful coexistence, mutual respect, and cooperation among Afghanistan's diverse communities.

Founded

The Council was founded in 2015 (1394).

Who we serve

Afghanistan's Sunni Hazara community.

Where we work

Across Afghanistan and among Afghan Sunni Hazara communities worldwide.
