Programs

The areas the Council works in.

I

Heritage & Culture

Safeguarding the community's cultural and historical heritage.

Oral-history interviews, a photo and document archive, and cultural evenings that keep songs, poems and stories alive.

Browse the archive

II

Solidarity

Strengthening solidarity and cooperation among Hazaras.

Welcome calls for new members, committees that anyone can join, and gatherings that bring families together.

Become a member

III

Rights & Voice

Advocating for the legitimate rights and interests of the community and creating opportunities for its constructive participation in society.

Community forums, member-led motions and information sessions on civic participation.

Member voting

IV

Unity of Afghanistan

Promoting unity, peaceful coexistence, mutual respect, and cooperation among Afghanistan's diverse communities.

Partnerships and shared events with other communities, built on mutual respect and cooperation.
