The National Council of Sunni Hazaras of Afghanistan is committed to preserving, strengthening, and promoting the ethnic, cultural, and social identity of Afghanistan's Sunni Hazara community.

From Afghanistan to Sunni Hazara communities around the world, the Council works for solidarity among Hazaras, advocates for the community's legitimate rights and interests, and opens opportunities for its constructive participation in society.
